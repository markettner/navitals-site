01 The map

A map that knows the trails

A detailed map in bright summer colours by day, with every gravel track, forest path and narrow trail on it. At night it turns dusky for city rides, or pitch black for the darkest forest.

  • Every trail and track, not just the roads
  • Overlays for official cycling and hiking routes, to follow one or plan along it
  • Summer colours by day; dusky or pitch black at night
  • A bold red route you can always pick out
The map: trails, forest paths and an official cycle route overlay
02 Navigation

Eyes on the trail, not the screen

Clear turn-by-turn directions on the map, spoken out loud, and on your Lock Screen. Take a wrong turn and Navitals guides you back to your route, or plans a new way from where you are, if you’d rather.

  • Spoken turn-by-turn directions
  • The next turn as a Live Activity on your Lock Screen
  • Off route? Back to your route, or a fresh one, your choice
Navigation: the next turn on a route through town
03 Vitals

Your heart rate, beside the next turn

Start a ride and your Apple Watch records it as a workout. Heart rate, zones, calories and the distance left sit right on the navigation screen, so you never have to switch apps to check on yourself.

  • Heart-rate zones based on your own resting and maximum heart rate
  • No watch? A Bluetooth chest strap works too
  • Every ride saved to Apple Health, with the weather you rode in
Navigation with heart rate, zones and calories
04 Planning

Routes made for the way you ride

Pick a profile and Navitals plans a route that suits it: quiet cycle paths for a day out, fast tarmac for the road bike, rough tracks for gravel and mountain bikes, or footpaths for a hike. Plan on your iPhone, or on your Mac, where you shape the route with a mouse.

Trekking Road Gravel MTB Hiking
  • Trekking, road, gravel, mountain bike and hiking profiles
  • Drag the route into shape on iPad or Mac, then ride it from your iPhone
  • Import a GPX file from anywhere, and export your own
A planned round trip with its distance and profile
05 Offline

Works where your signal doesn’t

Download the regions you ride in, and searching, planning and navigating all happen right on your iPhone. Deep in the forest, up in the mountains or across a border, nothing stops when the bars disappear.

  • Regions to download, suggested from where you are
  • Search for places with no signal
  • Plan, change and navigate routes fully offline
Downloading a region for offline use
06 Battery life

Designed to go easy on your battery

Your iPhone has to last the whole day, so saving power shaped how Navitals works. It doesn’t need the screen on to guide you, and when the screen is on, it asks less of it.

  • Screen off: spoken directions and the Live Activity guide you
  • Screen on: a lower refresh rate while navigating
  • A truly dark night theme that’s easy on the battery and your eyes
Navigating at night with the dark theme
07 Water lock

Rain doesn’t get to steer

Your iPhone handles rain just fine, its touchscreen less so. Raindrops pan the map, add stops and press buttons you never meant to, and that’s the last thing you need on a route you don’t know. Turn on the water lock and the screen ignores every drop while you keep navigating.

  • One tap locks the screen, navigation carries on
  • Slide to unlock when you actually need it
Navigation with the water lock on
