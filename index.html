The map

A map that knows the trails

A detailed map, with every gravel track, forest path and narrow trail on it, not just the roads. Switch on overlays for official cycling and hiking routes, ready to follow or to plan a ride along. Download the region you’re riding in, and the map and search keep working with no signal at all.

  • Every trail and track, not just the roads
  • Overlays for official cycling and hiking routes
  • Download a region, and it all works offline
The map: trails, forest paths and an official cycle route overlay
Navigation

Your route and your heart rate, on one screen

Turn-by-turn directions with the data from your Apple Watch right beside them: heart rate, zones, calories and the distance still to go. Routes are planned for the way you ride, whether that’s cycle paths, fast tarmac, gravel, singletrack or a hike. Plan them on your iPhone, even with no signal, or shape them with a mouse on your Mac.

  • Live heart rate, zones and calories from your Apple Watch
  • Routes for trekking, road, gravel and mountain bikes, and for hiking
  • Plan on iPhone, iPad or Mac, synced through iCloud
Navigation: the next turn, with heart rate and distance left
Battery life

Designed to go easy on your battery

Your iPhone has to last the whole day, so Navitals doesn’t need the screen on to guide you. Let it sleep and follow the spoken directions, or glance at the next turn on your Lock Screen. With the screen on, Navitals lowers its refresh rate while you ride. And at night, a truly dark theme is easy on the battery and on your eyes.

  • Spoken turn-by-turn directions
  • The next turn as a Live Activity on your Lock Screen
  • A lower refresh rate while navigating
  • A truly dark theme for night rides
The next turn as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen
